Early-Stage VCs & Accelerators (Australia – PetTech / VetTech Focus)
Artesian / SproutX – Leading AgTech-focused VC. Runs a dedicated accelerator and supports animal health and farm-to-vet solutions.
Skalata Ventures – Melbourne-based “seed-plus” investor, providing up to $1M follow-on funding. Strong portfolio in livestock health and digital marketplaces. Active in the Melbourne ecosystem.
Antler Australia – Pre-seed investor with residency programs in Melbourne and Sydney. Lists “pet services” as a portfolio interest.
Farmers2Founders – 2026 “Scale” Accelerator targets agrifood-tech and livestock/animal health startups. Applications typically open around April.
Giant Leap – Impact-focused fund. Suitable if Phone A Vet emphasises animal welfare or accessibility to care in regional areas.
Main Sequence Ventures – Focused on heavy R&D (AI diagnostics, deep data integration). Manages the CSIRO Innovation Fund and prioritises Animal Health.
Targeted Grants (2026)
LaunchVic Grants – Victorian Government grants for priority sectors including Agribusiness and Health Technologies. Includes “30X30” and sector-based pre-accelerator grants.
Industry Growth Program (IGP) – Federal program providing advisory and matched grants (up to $1M) to commercialise novel products.
CSIRO Kick-Start – Matched funding from $10,000 to $50,000 for R&D projects. Ideal for technical features like AI triage tools using CSIRO expertise.
Agricultural Traceability Grants (DAFF) – Supports projects assisting livestock health records or animal product traceability. Round 4 opened Jan 2026.
